2. Consent and withdrawal

On first launch the app shows this itemised notice and asks you to confirm you are 18 or older before you continue. You can withdraw consent as easily as you gave it: Settings > Privacy & consent > Withdraw ad personalisation or Withdraw all consent (one tap each). Withdrawal doesn't affect processing done before it. The notice is in English; translations into the 22 languages of the Eighth Schedule of the Constitution are being prepared.

5. Retention and erasure

Account data: until you delete the account, erased within 30 days of a request (in-app deletion is immediate in Firebase). On-device data: until uninstall. Purchase records: kept by Google Play as law requires.

6. Children

ClinCalc is an education app for adults (18+), mainly pharmacy, medical and nursing students. The app asks you to confirm you are 18+ before use. We don't knowingly process children's data, and we never show personalised (behavioural) ads unless 18+ is confirmed. If you believe a child has used the app, contact us and we will erase the data.

7. Security and breaches

Data in transit uses HTTPS/TLS; on-device data is protected by Android's sandbox. If a personal data breach occurs we will tell affected users without delay through the app or email, and notify the Data Protection Board of India without delay with a detailed report within 72 hours, and other regulators within their deadlines (e.g. 72 hours under GDPR).

8. Changes

We'll update this page and the effective date, and tell you in the app. A material change to the notice asks for consent again.
