ClinCalc Privacy Policy

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · App: ClinCalc (Android, package com.desreinstudios.clincalc) · Developer and data controller: Desrein Studios (Anuj Budhwar), India · Contact: anujbudhwar455@gmail.com

In short: calculations and interaction checks run on your device, and patient details and calculation entries are never uploaded. An account is optional. Guests see ads from Unity Ads; signed-in users and ClinCalc Pro subscribers don't. You can delete your account in the app at any time.

1. Data that stays on your device

Everything you type into a calculator or the interaction checker, your results, favourites, history and settings are stored only on your device. We have no access to them. They are removed when you uninstall the app or clear its data.

2. Optional account (Google Firebase Authentication)

If you choose to sign in with Google or with an email address and password, Google Firebase Authentication (Google LLC) processes your email address, name (Google sign-in only), and a user ID, plus technical sign-in data such as IP address and device information that Firebase needs for security. We use these only to sign you in, to remove ads for signed-in users and to link ClinCalc Pro to your account. Passwords are handled by Firebase, and we never see them. See Firebase privacy information.

3. Purchases (Google Play Billing)

ClinCalc Pro is sold through Google Play. Google processes payment. We receive only purchase status (product, time, renewal state and an order token) to unlock Pro. If you're signed in, the purchase carries a one-way hashed form of your user ID so it can be linked to your account. We never receive your card details.

4. Ads for guests (Unity Ads)

If you use ClinCalc without signing in, a banner ad from Unity Ads (Unity Technologies) appears on the Home, Search and Toolkit list screens. It never appears on calculators, results, interaction findings or the purchase screen. Unity may collect your device's advertising ID and other device identifiers, approximate location (derived from IP address), app interactions with ads (views and taps), diagnostics and limited purchase information, and may share them with its advertising partners to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. No health or patient information is shared with Unity. See the Unity privacy policy.

Where the law requires it, we ask your permission before showing personalised ads. You can choose non-personalised ads and change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad privacy choices. You can also reset or delete your advertising ID in your Android settings. Signing in removes ads completely.

6. Retention and deletion

Account data is kept until you delete your account. You can delete it in the app (Settings > Account > Delete account) or by following these steps. Deleting your account removes your Firebase Authentication record. Purchase records are kept by Google Play as required by law. Deleting the account doesn't cancel a Play subscription; cancel that in Google Play.

7. Children

ClinCalc is for healthcare professionals aged 18 and over. It isn't directed at children, and we don't knowingly collect children's data.

8. Security

All data sent by the app travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS/TLS). On-device data is protected by Android's app sandbox.

9. Changes

We'll update this page and the effective date if this policy changes, and we'll tell you in the app's What's new notice about any important change.
