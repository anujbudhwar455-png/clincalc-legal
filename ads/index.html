Ads & Your Choices

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · ClinCalc (Android, com.desreinstudios.clincalc) by Desrein Studios

Guests (not signed in, no Pro) see a banner from Unity Ads on the Home, Search and Toolkit list screens only, never on calculators, results, interaction findings or the purchase screen.

What Unity may collect

Advertising ID and device identifiers, approximate location from IP, ad views and taps, and diagnostics, to serve and measure ads and prevent fraud. No health or patient data is shared. See the Unity privacy policy.

Consent

In the EEA/UK/Switzerland, US, Brazil and India we ask before showing personalised ads. Personalised (behavioural) ads are never shown unless you have confirmed you are 18+.

Opt out

  • Settings > Ad privacy choices > Non-personalised only
  • Settings > Privacy & consent > Withdraw ad personalisation (one tap)
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings
  • Sign in (free) to remove ads completely
