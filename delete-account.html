Delete your ClinCalc account

ClinCalc by Desrein Studios. An account is optional. If you created one, you can delete it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ClinCalc and go to Settings > Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. If asked, sign in again first. This is a security check required before deletion.

Without the app

Email anujbudhwar455@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, with the subject "Delete my ClinCalc account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in record (email address, name, user ID and linked sign-in providers) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Not stored by us: calculations, favourites and history. These live only on your device and are removed when you uninstall the app.
  • Kept by Google Play: purchase and subscription records, as Google requires for tax and legal reasons. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription. Cancel it in Google Play > Payments & subscriptions.
