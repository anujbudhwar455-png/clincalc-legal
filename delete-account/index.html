Delete Your ClinCalc Account

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · ClinCalc (Android, com.desreinstudios.clincalc) by Desrein Studios

An account is optional. If you created one, you can delete it at any time.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open ClinCalc and go to Settings > Account.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.
  3. If asked, sign in again first (a security check).

Without the app

Email anujbudhwar455@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, subject "Delete my ClinCalc account". We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted and what is kept

  • Deleted: your sign-in record (email, name, user ID, linked providers) in Firebase Authentication.
  • Not stored by us: calculations, favourites and history live only on your device and are removed on uninstall.
  • Kept by Google Play: purchase records, as Google requires for tax and legal reasons. Deleting your account doesn't cancel a subscription.
