Subscriptions & Refunds

Effective date: 28 October 2026 · ClinCalc (Android, com.desreinstudios.clincalc) by Desrein Studios

Billing

ClinCalc Pro is billed by Google Play to the payment method on your Google account. The price and billing period are shown before you buy. Taxes may apply.

Auto-renewal

Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before renewal. Any free trial converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled before it ends.

Cancellation

Cancel anytime in Google Play > Profile > Payments & subscriptions > Subscriptions > ClinCalc. Pro stays active until the end of the paid period. Deleting the app or your ClinCalc account doesn't cancel the subscription.

Refunds

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policy. You may also contact us and we'll help where we can.

Restore

Sign in with the same Google Play account and use Restore purchases on the Pro screen.
