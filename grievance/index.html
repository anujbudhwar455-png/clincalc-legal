Your rights

  • Access a summary of your personal data and the processing, and the identities of processors it was shared with (Section 11)
  • Correct, complete, update or erase your data (Section 12)
  • Grievance redressal (Section 13): we respond within 90 days (Rule 14)
  • Nominate another person to exercise your rights in case of death or incapacity (Section 14)
  • Withdraw consent at any time, as easily as you gave it (Section 6(4)): Settings > Privacy & consent

How to make a request

Email the Grievance Officer with the account email (if any) and your request. We may ask you to confirm your identity. If you aren't satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
